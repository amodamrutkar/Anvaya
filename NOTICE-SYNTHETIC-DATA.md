NOTICE — SYNTHETIC DATA
=======================

All data, records, names, identifiers, account numbers, phone numbers, vehicle
registration numbers, case references, agency identifiers, events, and any other
information contained in this repository is ENTIRELY SYNTHETIC and FABRICATED.

This system was built as a hackathon demonstration prototype.

No real person is represented.
No real criminal case is represented.
No real law-enforcement agency record is represented.
No real financial account or transaction is represented.
No real organisation is represented.
No real incident is represented.

The synthetic dataset was generated programmatically using:
- Python Faker library (en_IN locale) for names and addresses
- Custom Marathi and Hindi name lexicons
- Seeded random number generators for reproducibility

All three "agency" data sources (STATE_POLICE, MAHA_CYBER, CENTRAL_AGENCY) are
fictional partitions of a single synthetic dataset. They do not represent real
agencies or real inter-agency data.

The Kumbh Mela scenario is entirely synthetic. No real event, location, or person
associated with Kumbh 2027 is represented.

Any resemblance to real persons, living or deceased, real cases, real organisations,
or real events is coincidental.

Legal parameters (e.g. MCOCA prerequisites) are included as configurable placeholders
and are marked `verified: false` until confirmed by a qualified legal source. They
must not be stated as legal fact.

Simulated connectors (SimulatedCCTNS, SimulatedMahaCrimeOS, SimulatedMahaCyber,
SimulatedNATGRID) do not connect to any real system. All outputs are labelled
`simulated: true`.
